Why every behavioural instrument is flat
  The term changes the bot's top-1 on 13.8% of decisions (action class on 8.5%).
  The SEED ALONE changes it on 15.5% at the same budget. The behavioural
  footprint is smaller than the search's own noise, so no downstream instrument
  can resolve it, and the duel reads parity by construction.

Verdict: NOT SHIPPED. Default stays exchange = 0.0. The only demonstrated
effect is on an offline fit statistic, and eval01 is never displayed to the
user (grep of web/src: no win-probability surface), so better calibration has
no user-visible channel except through decisions that do not measurably change.
Resolving the pooled +0.007 would need ~20,000 games, about 12 hours of VM.

Read only rows with certwidth and entrygap ~0: a mixture solved off a
bracketed payoff matrix is not an equilibrium of anything. delta < 0 means
the bot switches LESS than equilibrium (its disagreement with humans would
then be the bot's error); delta ~ 0 means the bot is right and the human
switch rate is the thing that needs explaining. Rows flagged UNUSABLE say
nothing either way — a decided position makes every strategy optimal, so its
equilibrium switch rate is an artifact of which vertex the LP happened to pick.

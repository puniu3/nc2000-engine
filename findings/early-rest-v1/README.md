# Narrow unrevealed Rest candidate

The production policy remains EarlyPerish. EarlyRest is opt-in through
`Belief::with_fallback_policy` and the research driver. It keeps the existing
Perish draw when present. Otherwise, for an appeared living fallback mon
without modeled Rest, it samples a Rest-inserted version of the same
imputation roster. Its probability is the compatible fixed-pool Rest
source weight divided by total compatible source weight. Known moves,
Mean Look and Perish Song cannot be displaced. Other stats/items/DVs
remain unchanged. Full or unsupported observations keep the original path.

Source compatibility requires species, public item presence/original item,
all revealed moves, and level-legal complete source moves. Source DVs and
gender are not transferred: the candidate changes an unrevealed move slot
only. The probability is a fixed prior assumption, not fitted to these
outcomes or calibrated as human Rest prevalence.

## Pilot

The protocol was frozen before outcomes. Four constructed families each
have Rest and no-Rest actual lead kits, with four paired trials per case.
Both arms have identical battle/search seeds, alternate sides, use 27,000
iterations and c=0.4, and face the same EarlyPerish opponent. An unselected
level change forces off-pool inference. Every full team and forced prefix
was validated before any match. Two additional on-pool control pairs test
identity. Hidden Power aliases use their DV-derived plain moves; the HP
inference experiment stays disabled.

All 68 games ended. Across 32 stress pairs, production scored 12 and the
candidate 13: four improved pairs, three worsened and 25 tied. Rest cases
were 6–6; no-Rest cases 6–7. Both on-pool pairs have identical trajectories.
This is not convincing evidence of the intended recovery benefit.

The analysis plan was recorded while games ran, before inspecting any
outcomes. It uses a symmetric Dirichlet joint paired-outcome model per
case, averaging the eight cases equally. At prior mass 1 per case,
P(improvement) is 65.19%, posterior mean +2.51 percentage points,
95% credible interval [-10.50, +15.34] points. Varying prior mass from 0.5
to 64 gives 66.74% to 53.03%. It does not meet the owner's previously used
80% model-conditional threshold. No strength or expert-human claim follows.

The positive pilot point estimate permits a fixed fresh confirmation,
recorded separately in `early-rest-confirmation-v1`: eight new seed pairs
per stress case. Its results alone determine the candidate decision;
pilot outcomes are not pooled into the primary confirmation analysis.

## Checks and scope

- 152 bot unit tests and ten position tests passed; workspace/all-targets
  compilation passed.
- Tests check source-weighted frequency, only replacing an unknown slot,
  spent PP, metadata/DV/stat invariance, no effect before appearance or
  after fainting, fully revealed/unsupported cases, item contradictions,
  and both existing Perish sampling and retained Perish-slot preservation.
- The pilot began before adding the final retained-Perish slot guard.
  `perish-guard-equivalence.json` identifies every selected opposing species
  and proves the protected moves cannot enter their imputation references
  in these fixtures. The shared opponent stays production. Final-code
  replay and every first-divergence search are checked independently.
- All 68 games and 1,775 decisions replay exactly. All 54 searches at the
  27 first-divergence positions reproduce their recorded choices.
- The human diagnostic retains exactly the same 6,701 labels and baseline
  model rows. Rest support increases from 123/436 to 332/436, but average
  per-sample support over all newly selected moves falls slightly. This
  positive-label diagnostic motivated the candidate and is not independent
  strength evidence.

The fixed half-Rest/half-no-Rest mixture is a stress benchmark, not a human
population estimate. Raw spectator logs and player handles are excluded.
The archive contains anonymous diagnostic rows and generated match traces.

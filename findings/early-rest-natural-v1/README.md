# Unforced archived-rental robustness screen

EarlyRest failed the predeclared reversal screen: EarlyPerish scored
11/24 and EarlyRest 8/24. There were three improved, six worsened and
15 tied pairs. P(delta < 0) = 85.2335% under the fixed mass-1 model,
above the 80% reversal threshold. The local default returns to EarlyPerish;
EarlyRest remains a research option. Public deployment was never changed.

The posterior mean is -9.97 percentage points with 95% credible interval
[-28.43, +8.32]. Prior masses 0.5, 1, 4, 16, 64 give worsening probabilities
86.99%, 85.23%, 78.60%, 68.46%, 60.07%. The reversal follows the frozen
primary criterion, not a claim of certainty across all reasonable models.
These outcomes are not pooled with the earlier constructed benchmark.

All 52 games terminated. Both on-pool control pairs have identical
trajectories. All 1,997 decisions replay with matching legal choices,
state keys, seeds and outcomes. All 42 first-divergence searches reproduce.
A pair changing its first move does not establish the cause of its outcome.

## Sampling and scope

The native inventory audit found 85 eligible human-origin teams: 82 have
at least one current prior candidate compatible with preview, all moves
and original items. Three rentals have no preview candidate because of
public gender differences. Their moves/items overlap known source teams.
They are useful unchanged off-pool tests, not independent human holdouts.
The maximal move/item audit is set compatibility, not a played trajectory.

All three rentals are crossed with the two highest-weight catalog teams,
using catalog order to break ties: sample-17-orig and rental-cban-13.
Each of six cases has four paired fresh seeds with alternating sides.
Two on-pool control pairs are separate. Teams are unaltered except native
Hidden Power alias normalization, preserving original DVs. Native full-team
validation runs before any game. No picks or opening moves are forced.
Both arms use 27,000 iterations, c=0.4, and a common EarlyPerish opponent.
The 24 stress pairs alone form the equal-weight six-case estimand.
All opposing rosters contain Rest; no-Rest-roster results are unmeasurable.

The protocol, analysis plan and candidate source hashes were committed at
241cdaa before outcomes. The fixed sample was neither extended nor reduced.
Run tools/early-rest-natural-report against those frozen candidate sources
and the original temporary games to replay and aggregate; source/binary
hashes are checked. The archive stores full generated match records.

## Human-evidence boundary

The archived 190 bot-account logs contain 88 wins, 96 losses and six
without a terminal record. There are 21 opponent account names, which
are not 21 independently identified people or verified experts. A prior
historical clustering analysis suggests strong account dependence; this
census neither verifies nor rejects that attribution. No recognized build
marker ties these old logs to the current candidate. They cannot establish
current EarlyRest performance against expert humans. Only anonymous
aggregate evidence and content fingerprints are exported here.

## Consequence

Better support for unseen human moves and a positive constructed matchup
benchmark did not carry into this separate unforced screen. Retain the
previous policy. Neither result proves that the bot is near optimal or
above expert humans. Any future candidate must survive ordinary-play
checks before changing the default; do not rescue this one by pooling
samples or tuning against its losing games.

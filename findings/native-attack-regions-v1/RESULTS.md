# Complete kernels over preserved KO regions

The prototype reuses a complete attack distribution only within an HP rectangle preserving the survival/KO pattern of every source leaf. Surviving leaf HP translates by its damage delta; a defeated active remains at zero and contributes an upper HP bound at the source HP. Terminal reuse is disabled whenever the existing engine guard says damage bookkeeping is observable. Different regions of the same semantic input have separate FIFO entries. Outside a stored region, ordinary complete enumeration runs.

All 208 two-dimensional HP-grid comparisons across both orientations agree with independent engine enumeration. Separate tests cover observable Counter history and the turn cap. The prior 80 admitted move/item/status conditions, storage/cap/eviction checks and survivor-distribution comparisons passed. This does not broaden the existing admitted move/item domain.

On the same frozen 96 native successor cases, all 768 distributions in the eight-pass control/scripts/kernel/regions/regions/kernel/scripts/control comparison have identical guarded probability-map hashes. Region reuse hits 36 of 96 calls, with 48 region misses, 28 FIFO evictions and a peak of 16,381 retained leaves. Actual engine executions fall from 35,815 to 29,781 (about 16.8%).

Mean measured enumeration time is 440.0 ms versus 499.5 ms for control, an exploratory 11.9% reduction. Each arm has two serial passes including cold preparation. This is a small conditional microbenchmark: only 12 of 768 representative legal action pairs from two of six roots pass the original admission guard. It does not establish whole-solver or ordinary-bot speed, stronger play, or iPad performance. Next validation is optional integration in the isolated analysis solver.

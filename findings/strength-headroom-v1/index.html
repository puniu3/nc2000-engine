Residual waiting hole fixed locally

The patch preserves waits under visible residual damage while keeping self-KO and sleep-forfeit guards. All 24 native decisions now choose a certified winning wait with unchanged search statistics. The Wasm bridge also wins all 16 tested continuations. Publication has not been performed.

Fix verification · Corrected measurements

Original diagnosis before the fix

The previous bot could search out a certain win, then discard it at the final action filter. This is a proven local defect. Its effect on average playing strength is not yet measured.

The counterexample

Turn 8: Jolteon has 9/171 HP. Wobbuffet has 71/296 HP, Leftovers, and escalating poison. Its next poison tick deals 108 HP. Jolteon is faster, and neither side has a bench.

Using Toxic or Thunder Wave fails to add another status, but safely waits for poison to win. Return risks Counter; Thunder Shock risks Mirror Coat. The opponent cannot save itself with Safeguard or last-mon Destiny Bond.

This is an adversarial, reduced one-mon-per-side endgame built by seven legal engine turns. No HP, status, PP or clock was injected into the proof position. It is not a recorded ladder game or a six-mon preview fixture. The construction log is below.

The search already knows

At the live budget of 27,000 iterations and c = 0.4, more than 99.8% of visits go to the two waiting moves, both with search mean 1.0. The action filter labels both “the target already carries a major status” and excludes them. All 24 searches select an attack.

The public-protocol test feeds only Jolteon’s player stream, its own set, and its request into ProtocolAgent with the shipped v3 prior. It repeats the defect on eight fresh seeds. The other sixteen searches cover pinned and blind direct search. These are repetitions of one position, not 24 independent games.

Simply reading the highest visit count before this filter chooses a proven winning wait in every run, at zero extra search cost. That is a causal ablation for this position, not a validated recommendation to disable every mask globally.

Why the existing gate misses it

noop_census checks whether a move itself succeeds. Toxic really does fail here, so that gate would confirm the no-op classification. What is false is the inference from “no direct effect” to “safe to remove from the policy.” Turn timing and the opponent’s response have value.

The recent Destiny Bond and Perish Song exceptions already protect two forms of waiting. Poison plus retaliation exposes the same underlying assumption outside those exceptions.

How large is the opportunity?

The archive contains 570 games: none with Wobbuffet, eight with Counter, seven with Mirror Coat, and eight with a retaliation move plus toxic status somewhere in the same game. These are exposure counts, not affected-position counts. This example refutes a blanket optimality claim; it does not establish a large ladder win-rate gain.

The local correction now retains waiting actions during visible residual damage, with terminal-outcome checks and unchanged self-KO/sleep-forfeit protection. Measuring its effect on broader blind match results remains separate work. Do not infer strength from a zero no-op disagreement count.

These reject particular approaches under their test conditions. None bounds the benefit of every possible policy change. The new counterexample shows a defect that persists regardless of search budget.

Checks and reproduction

Removing poison in a control removes the immediate winning certificate: waiting becomes unresolved, not falsely labelled losing. Reducing Wobbuffet to 1 HP makes all attacks win too, and the waiting advantage vanishes. These controls change one field deliberately; the main proof is reached only through moves.

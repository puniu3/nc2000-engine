The final proof is final-proof-v2. Earlier exploratory directories are retained as failed or deliberately looser bounds, and do not support the reported conclusion. The final direct proof counts pure wins, treats draws as losses, and allows every legal opposing action. The Curse upper proof leaves every future own action free. Native/WASM parity is a sampled compatibility check, not a proof for every chance branch. Use the full repository for compiling; this evidence bundle is not a standalone source distribution.

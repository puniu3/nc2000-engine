All ten roots absent from the earlier 70-root loss-lower screen completed the
registered run. Eight exhausted their 250,000-execution budgets; two reached
the 50,000-node stopping threshold. Total engine executions: 2,286,805.
All ten remain unresolved. None has a positive selected-action loss lower bound,
none is strictly dominated by the matrix intervals, and none newly satisfies
the loss-at-most-0.02 threshold. Combined original coverage remains 41 small-loss
roots and 319 unresolved out of 360.

The frozen helper and binary were reused, with their hashes verified. Every
target is the actual archived ordinary bot action. Source selection is recomputed
from the first-step nominees minus the earlier deep-screen roots; all ten are
retained. Cell and value intervals overlap the initial screen, and all matrices
pass the independent rational LP checker. Missing roots, changed targets, false
threshold results and invalid cells are rejected.

One-step winning probability was used only to choose where to inspect. Higher
immediate winning probability does not establish lower long-term loss when the
selected move heals, boosts or inflicts status. This follow-up confirms that
candidate selection alone did not overcome the continuation-analysis limit.
No ordinary bot, engine rule, sleep-counter semantics or public deployment changed.
These are fixed-hidden-world, full-information continuation results, not a
certificate of a blind-policy mistake or proximity to global optimality.

NC2000

Overview

A Pokémon Gen 2 battle bot for Nintendo Cup 2000. It plays on par with experienced players and takes approximately three seconds per move on local hardware.

Play ↗Code ↗

Technology

  • Rust rules engine. Reimplemented from Pokémon Showdown, with battle state, random-number state and logs checked against the reference implementation.
  • Search-based play. Monte Carlo tree search evaluates actions through simulated continuations. Unrevealed opposing moves and items are sampled from candidates consistent with observations.
  • Local solvers. Bounded analysis of small positions, supplemented by checks that reject provably ineffective actions.
  • No neural network in the deployed bot. Several approaches were tested; none established a playing-strength improvement over the search baseline.
